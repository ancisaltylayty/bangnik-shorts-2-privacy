## 1. Privacy Policy

### About the App

**Bangnik Shorts 2** ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is an Instagram automation tool. It listens to Instagram comment webhooks on connected Instagram Professional accounts and sends automated Private Reply direct messages (DMs) when a comment matches a configured keyword rule.

The App works only through the official **Meta Instagram Graph API** and follows the [Meta Platform Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms/) and [Developer Policies](https://developers.facebook.com/devpolicy/).

### Data We Collect

When someone comments on a post owned by a connected Instagram account, Meta sends a webhook event to the App. From that event, the App processes only the following data:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Comment text | To check whether the comment matches a keyword rule |
| Comment ID | To send a Private Reply to the correct comment |
| Media ID | To find which post the comment belongs to and which keyword rules apply |
| Commenter's Instagram username and user ID | To address and deliver the automated Private Reply |

The App does **not** collect passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, location data, contacts, or any other personal data.

### How We Use Data

We use the data above **only** to:

1. Match comment text against the keyword rules set by the connected account owner.
2. Send an automated Private Reply DM to the commenter through the Instagram Graph API.

We do **not** use this data for advertising, profiling, analytics resale, AI model training, or any purpose other than delivering the automated reply.

### Data Sharing

- We **do not sell** any user data.
- We **do not share** user data with third parties.
- Data is sent only to Meta through the official Instagram Graph API, and only to deliver the Private Reply.
- We may disclose data only if applicable law or a valid legal process requires it.

### Data Retention

- Comment data is processed **temporarily**, only for as long as needed to match keyword rules and deliver the automated reply.
- We do not keep a permanent database of comments, commenters, or messages.
- Any temporary technical records (for example, short-lived error logs used to keep the service running) are deleted automatically within **30 days**.
- When a user asks for deletion, we delete their data within **48 hours** (see [Section 2](#2-user-data-deletion-instructions)).

### Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data while it is processed. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, webhook signature verification, and restricted access to access tokens and server systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

### Your Rights

You may ask to access or delete your data, or object to its processing, at any time. To do this, follow the [User Data Deletion Instructions](#2-user-data-deletion-instructions) below or email us.

### Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by Instagram in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.

## 2. User Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask to have all data related to you deleted from **Bangnik Shorts 2** at any time. Use either of the options below.

### Option A — Request Deletion by Email

1. Send an email to **[hello@bangnik.com](mailto:hello@bangnik.com)**.
2. Use the subject line: **`Data Deletion Request – Bangnik Shorts 2`**
3. In the email body, include your **Instagram username** so we can find your data.
4. We will delete all data related to your Instagram account **within 48 hours** of receiving your request.
5. We will email you to confirm when the deletion is complete.

### Option B — Remove App Access in Instagram Settings

1. Open the **Instagram** app or go to [instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com).
2. Go to your **Profile** → tap the **menu (☰)** → **Settings and activity**.
3. Open **Apps and websites**.
4. Under **Active**, find **Bangnik Shorts 2**.
5. Tap **Remove** and confirm.

After you remove access, the App can no longer receive or process your data. Any remaining data related to your account will be deleted **within 48 hours**.

> **Note:** Instagram's menu labels may change slightly over time. If you can't find the option, use **Option A** (email) instead.

### Our Deletion Commitment

- All data related to your request is deleted **within 48 hours**.
- Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
- If you have any questions about your deletion request, contact **[hello@bangnik.com](mailto:hello@bangnik.com)**.
